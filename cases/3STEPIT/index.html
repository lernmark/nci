The opportunity

Make every device worth more.

Businesses need reliable technology, but devices are often replaced before their useful life is over. 3stepIT helps customers manage equipment as a lifecycle, extending use and recovering value at each stage.

The circular model

Leasing makes reuse part of the business model.

Through lifecycle management and leasing, 3stepIT helps customers keep equipment in use for longer. When a device is returned, it can be assessed, refurbished and redeployed, with end-of-life equipment responsibly recovered.

An ecosystem that creates value from every device

Foxway helps organisations use laptops, phones and other IT equipment as a service. It takes devices back, tests and refurbishes them, and returns them to the market. Instead of treating technology as disposable, Foxway builds value from several useful lives of the same device.

The opportunity

The next device doesn’t always need to be a new one.

Organisations need reliable technology, predictable costs and secure handling of their equipment. Foxway built a service around that need: supply devices, manage them during use, bring them back at the right time, and prepare them for another customer.

1. Customer need

Businesses need laptops and phones that work, without managing every device themselves.

2. Return flow

Service and leasing contracts create a predictable route for devices to come back.

3. Second life

Testing, repair and refurbishment make suitable devices ready for another user.

The circular model

Keep the value in the device, not just the device in circulation.

Foxway’s model works by managing the full journey of a device. The crucial skill is knowing when to take it back and what to do next, so it can be used again without spending more than the recovered value justifies.

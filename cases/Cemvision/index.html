The opportunity

Laser focus for a massive climate challenge

Cement accounts for approximately 7 - 8% of global CO₂ emissions. Cemvision is targeting this specific problem with Re-ment. Its opportunity is to replace conventional cement in established construction applications without requiring customers to redesign their production processes or pay a green premium.

The circular model

Turning steel slag into cement feedstock and valuable metals

Cemvision creates value across two industrial value chains: steelmaking and construction. Its technology upgrades low-grade slag into cement feedstock while recovering valuable metals, such as vanadium, that can be returned to industrial use.

By replacing virgin raw materials with industrial by-products, the process aims to reduce emissions, material waste and dependence on newly extracted resources.

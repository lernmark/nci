Privacy policy

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit this website or request a case PDF, sign up or register for an event through it.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the organisation behind this website, organisation number 559369-8854. Questions about your personal data can be sent to info@fundforward.se.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in our forms: your name, your business and your email address.
  • Which case you requested, so we know what you are interested in.
  • Technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request, which our hosting provider processes in order to deliver the website and receive form submissions.

Why we use your data

  • To provide the case PDF you asked for and to follow up on your interest in the Nordic Circular Investments cases.
  • To invite you to events.
  • To operate and secure the website.

Our legal basis is your consent when you submit a form (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in running and securing the website (Article 6(1)(f)). You can withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect processing that happened before.

How long we keep it

We keep the details you submit for 24 months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Who receives your data

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which also receives and stores form submissions on our behalf. Netlify may process data outside the EU/EEA, in which case it relies on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

Some pages link to external services, for example Zoom for our online events. When you follow such a link you leave this website, and the service's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and styles are served from our own site, so your browser does not contact third parties when you view the pages.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Contact us at info@fundforward.se to use these rights. If you believe we handle your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
